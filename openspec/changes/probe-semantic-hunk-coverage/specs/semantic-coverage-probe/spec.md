## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Predeclared label-independent coverage probe
The system SHALL seal fixed hunk-stratum quotas and SHA-ordered train/validation members before new context or model execution, bind immutable inputs and sources, and retain full-population impact and all hard cases. It SHALL NOT select using labels, scores, oldtest or subsequent results.

#### Scenario: Valid fixed population
- **WHEN** a sealed660-record train/validation parent has sufficient declared strata
- **THEN** exactly the declared at-most64/32 identities are chosen reproducibly and the declaration precedes computation

#### Scenario: Invalid population
- **WHEN** duplicate identities, wrong partitions, overflow, drift or insufficient strata occur
- **THEN** the probe fails without silently substituting easier records or touching old assets

### Requirement: Bounded traceable whole-line windows
The probe SHALL reproduce selected v2 contexts from bound blobs, preserve every v2 selected whole line and unchanged old pipelines, and use a separately versioned1008-token whole-line code-role policy with at most8 windows of128 tokens and unchanged message policy. Whole-line hunk representation SHALL remain distinct from full-hunk, full-change and model-utility claims.

#### Scenario: Larger budget displaces earlier coverage
- **WHEN** naive greedy expansion could consume the budget with a long line
- **THEN** the candidate seeds every exactly reconstructed v2 line before expansion and refuses any loss of those coordinates

#### Scenario: Partial or capped coverage
- **WHEN** a long line, hunk cap, candidate-line cap or budget prevents complete coverage
- **THEN** original and selected counts and omissions remain explicit and no population member or readiness failure is hidden

#### Scenario: Frozen pooling and bounded execution
- **WHEN** the offline candidate is encoded
- **THEN** safe frozen weights, content-only masking, token-weighted short-tail pooling, role masks and full provenance are verified under CPU/time/RSS/capacity limits, and all selected vectors are checked in an independent repeat

### Requirement: Preserve product qualification and environment boundaries
Engineering probe success SHALL NOT certify oldtest utility, professional review, licensing, full-population coverage or product release. TUN compatibility SHALL depend on actual public DNS and application connectivity while retaining SSRF restrictions.

#### Scenario: Proxy returns non-public mapping
- **WHEN** a public repository resolves to a Fake-IP or private address
- **THEN** existing rejection remains and environment evidence is recorded without weakening safety or changing global network settings
