## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Predeclared native population and honest observation
The system SHALL bind a fixed native source, original acquisition time, label-independent identity selection and fixed temporal roles before deriving new outcomes. It SHALL distinguish provisional silver observations, Unknown, actual knowledge and counterfactual availability.

#### Scenario: Fixed new population
- **WHEN** the verified source and original identity exclusion are declared
- **THEN** all eligible2020–2025 single-parent targets retain their identity and year role, the reserved64 remains evaluation-only, and no old test label or model score selects members or roles

#### Scenario: Valid link and complete negative observation
- **WHEN** real supported SZZ lines verify strict ancestry, original blob/line identity and inclusive W90 time bounds
- **THEN** provisional compatible positives retain their line evidence, and a provisional negative requires complete declared observation with no unresolved gap throughout its horizon
- **AND** neither outcome certifies all real bugs, independent human review, Gold or product compatibility

#### Scenario: Missing and partial observations
- **WHEN** merge/root, encoding, binary/content, filter, blame, missing objects, inversion or resource limits prevent complete observation
- **THEN** gaps and affected Unknown outcomes remain explicit, no unsupported target is dropped or relabelled clean, and insufficient class/support gates refuse training

### Requirement: Bounded reproducible source evidence
The system SHALL use bounded supervised local Git, immutable source/execution bindings and exclusive publication. It SHALL preserve failed attempts and verify completed units before reuse.

#### Scenario: Interruption and independent recovery
- **WHEN** an operator process fails or is interrupted and later resumes with the same bindings
- **THEN** only verified completed units may be reused, no partial final result is published, and an independent restored root can verify the final evidence without changing original acquisition times

#### Scenario: Drift or unsafe input
- **WHEN** source/code/member/receipt/unit content changes or paths, sizes, shapes, deadlines or cancellation violate the declared contract
- **THEN** the operation refuses publication, preserves prior artifacts and does not execute repository code, hooks, remote code or hidden network fetches

### Requirement: Explicit weight evidence and qualification boundaries
The system SHALL bind authoritative fixed-revision small-file evidence separately from downloaded weights, serialization safety, representation, license obligations, contamination and task eligibility.

#### Scenario: Official candidate metadata
- **WHEN** actual official metadata/card/config bytes and Git identities match the declared revision
- **THEN** their observed license and expected LFS identity are recorded with actual acquisition times, but absent weight/safe-conversion/compatibility evidence remains absent and cannot authorize training or distribution

#### Scenario: Legacy and product preservation
- **WHEN** the new audit is implemented or passes engineering tests
- **THEN** old labels/splits/models/semantic assets, A/B evidence and qualification flags are preserved, APIs/schema remain unchanged, and native14/history/candidate/eight-model/semantic-language/governance/demo requirements remain independently open
