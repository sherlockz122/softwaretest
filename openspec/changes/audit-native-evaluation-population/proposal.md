# Audit a new native evaluation population

## Why

The old 660 embeddings have no new evaluation labels, the exposed test cannot select another model, and the old threshold group has only two positives. Reliable asset tasks do not solve these scientific and product dependencies. A fresh fixed-source population must have explicit target, clock, observation, support and missing-evidence audits before training.

## What Changes

- Declare ActiveMQ's already acquired main-reachable fixed HEAD, all eligible single-parent 2020–2025 commits outside the old 106674 identities, and fixed year roles before reading new outcomes. The old reserved64 can occur only in the 2025 evaluation role and remains unchanged.
- Independently bind native commit/blob identity, current message Fix rules and bounded baseline SZZ line evidence, with observation through 2026-04-01 and W90/L7 counterfactual policy. Preserve unsupported/partial observations and Unknown; do not assert all defects or historical actual knowledge.
- Produce immutable source/outcome/support/qualification evidence and an independently reproducible operator CLI. Actual source completeness and sample support can fail; no classifier, old-test score, new embedding or product release is authorized by this audit.
- Bind official fixed-revision UniXcoder Apache-2.0 card/config evidence as a candidate alternative, separately from actual weight acquisition, safe conversion, representation compatibility and distribution approval.
