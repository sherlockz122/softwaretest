## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Immutable complete-case source supplement
The system SHALL bind all original84 gap cases and the full fixed population to a new source version, retaining original acquisition, roles, reserved identities, payloads and qualification boundaries.

#### Scenario: All declared cases
- **WHEN** the operator declares and resolves the bound stage32 input
- **THEN** all84 observations and required target evidence have explicit dispositions, all1120 members and1971 observations remain represented, and failures or unresolved cases remain gaps rather than absent rows or clean labels

#### Scenario: Legacy drift or unsafe inputs
- **WHEN** original/current code, inputs, members, receipt, path, source objects, cached units or output constraints drift
- **THEN** publication is refused, prior evidence remains intact, and no repository code, hooks, hidden network or untrusted deserialization executes

### Requirement: Explicit all-parent evidence and uncertainty
The system SHALL verify every supported original parent edge and line/blob identity before refining a merge gap, and SHALL distinguish parent consensus, concrete ambiguity and unmapped evidence.

#### Scenario: Consensus origin
- **WHEN** an eligible origin is genuinely witnessed with valid times and strict ancestry on every original parent edge
- **THEN** the new policy may emit a provisional consensus-silver positive with every edge's line evidence, without claiming causal Gold or altering the old single-parent result

#### Scenario: Divergent parents or missing evidence
- **WHEN** concrete parent origins disagree or target eligibility is unavailable
- **THEN** affected origins remain target gaps, never negative by lack of consensus
- **AND** unmapped files/lines, incomplete parent edges, unsupported encodings/filters or unverifiable outside identities remain global gaps; empty combined output cannot certify complete observation

#### Scenario: Horizon reconstruction
- **WHEN** new evidence is projected over the original W90/L7 horizon
- **THEN** every target keeps its role and actual knowledge remains distinct from reconstructed availability; a provisional negative requires complete applicable observation with no unresolved gap
- **AND** independent reconstruction must match all results; source counts or engineering success do not grant training, historical, candidate or product readiness

### Requirement: Bounded owned-process execution and validated caches
The new runtime SHALL retain supervision and final output/deadline checks, clean only owned process identities, and bind bounded caches to immutable source identities.

#### Scenario: Inner timeout output limit or cancellation
- **WHEN** a real nested child exceeds a deadline/output bound, supervision is revoked, or Windows taskkill fails
- **THEN** owned handle/tree cleanup still runs, temporary streams are handled, incomplete cleanup fails explicitly, and no successful evidence is published

#### Scenario: Normal and cached results
- **WHEN** normal commands or cache hits remain within original bounds
- **THEN** complete bytes/statuses/line facts and missing reasons match the declared reference, hit paths still check supervision, errors are not cached, and incompatible source/mode identities cannot share entries

#### Scenario: Nested target budget isolation
- **WHEN** a fresh target parse runs inside an observation trace under source versionv3
- **THEN** its metadata/record reads keep their own30-second bounds, the parent's remaining active budget is preserved after the suboperation's actual elapsed time, and cached lookup work remains charged to the parent
- **AND** whole-phase wall/RSS/cancellation/output checks remain active, fatal errors still abort without publication, and cold/warm execution must reproduce every line fact, missing reason and target gap list rather than merely label totals

#### Scenario: Performance adoption
- **WHEN** the fixed two-unit, original-eligibility benchmark completes three alternating pairs
- **THEN** full observations are compared, all runs/resources/cleanup are recorded, and caches are adopted only on at least20% matched median improvement and successful safety/equality checks; otherwise the uncached path and negative result remain

### Requirement: Reproducible bounded delivery
The system SHALL preserve interruption evidence, verify resumed units, account for temporary growth and reproduce the new closure independently without changing old assets.

#### Scenario: Resume and independent root
- **WHEN** a failed run resumes or another root replays the copied evidence closure from an empty cache
- **THEN** original bindings/times are revalidated, partial output is not called final, logical payloads and all target outcomes reproduce, and unsupported evidence stays explicit

#### Scenario: Engineering and product separation
- **WHEN** full current engineering, normal-data/model/review protection, ordinary storage guards and both own-SHA CIs pass
- **THEN** a bounded batch delivery may be recorded, while actual unresolved source/support/professional-review/representation/model and historical/candidate business gates remain open
